## Introduction

I'm **Jing-Ping Yu** (hicat0x0), currently a graduate student in the
**Master Program in Information Security (MPIS)** at
**National Taipei University of Technology**.
I received my B.S. in **Computer Science and Information Engineering**
from **National University of Tainan** in 2026.

My work centres on **offensive and defensive security**,
**wireless communications and SDR**, **cloud and virtualisation systems**,
and forward-looking topics such as **quantum computing**.
During my undergraduate years I was active in the Taiwanese security
community and in CTF competitions, building hands-on experience in
vulnerability analysis, reverse engineering and system defence.

I like turning the concepts I read about into something that actually runs,
and I use this blog to record technical notes, CTF write-ups
and the things that went wrong along the way.
